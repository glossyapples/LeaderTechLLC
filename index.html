About

Built independently. Designed practically.

Leadertech LLC is an independent technology company creating software, games, and automation tools. Our products are designed around useful workflows, creative experimentation, and fast iteration.

We focus on products that can be built, tested, shipped, and improved — not endless pitch decks or theoretical platforms. Every project starts with a simple question: can this make something easier, more useful, or more fun?

  • Software Tools

    Focused utilities that automate repetitive work, simplify complex workflows, and help people move faster.

  • Games

    Playable worlds and interactive experiences built around exploration, experimentation, and personality.

  • AI & Automation

    Agentic workflows that assist with research, publishing, strategy, and decision support.

  • Creative Products

    Tools and experiences designed for creators, small businesses, and independent operators.
